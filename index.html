Why the August drop happened

Suppressions were applied across August and early September while a machine/bot open tracking problem was investigated. Sends fell from ~17M/week to ~12–14M/week from 24 August.

The fault sits with Iterable's tracking, not Bark's deliverability. Suppressions have been removed and volume should return to the July baseline. Iterable are working on a fix and may backdate the missing data.

So plan against the trailing-12-month figure and re-check the run-rate in four weeks, once unsuppressed volume has washed through.

Term and monthly allowances fail differently

Email, WhatsApp and push are one pot for the year. A heavy month is fine if the year totals under — which is why these need a forecast, not a monthly check.

Profiles, custom events and embedded messaging reset monthly, and profiles are a daily high-water mark, so one day's spike sets that month's bill and a quiet month can't offset it.

Everything ends 14 Aug 2027 and does not auto-renew. Allowance can be added mid-term with 30 days' notice; it can't be reduced.
