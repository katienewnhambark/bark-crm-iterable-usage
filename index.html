Summer-level volume breaches the allowance; spring-level volume doesn't

The break-even rate is 2,142,466 sends/day. June ran 2.52M, July 2.63M and the clean days since suppressions lifted 2.56M — all 117–123% of allowance. February to April ran 1.89–2.02M, or 88–94%.

So the 101.8% trailing-12-month figure is an average of two different regimes, not a steady state. The year's outcome turns on which one holds — not on anything in the suppression window.

Do this: plan against 796M for now, and re-cut after four clean weeks (w/c 12 Oct). If the daily rate stays above 2.14M, the year goes over — and buying allowance at committed rates with 30 days' notice beats paying $0.40 CPM in arrears.

Term and monthly allowances fail differently

Email, WhatsApp and push are one pot for the year. A heavy month is fine if the year totals under — which is why these need a forecast, not a monthly check.

Profiles, custom events and embedded messaging reset monthly, and profiles are a daily high-water mark, so one day's spike sets that month's bill and a quiet month can't offset it.

Everything ends 14 Aug 2027 and does not auto-renew. Allowance can be added mid-term with 30 days' notice; it can't be reduced.
