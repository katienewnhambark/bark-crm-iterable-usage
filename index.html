Term, cycle and carryover all behave differently

Email, WhatsApp and push are one pot for the year — a heavy month is fine if the year totals under, which is why they need a forecast rather than a monthly check.

Profiles, custom events and embedded messaging reset each 15th–14th cycle, and profiles are a daily high-water mark, so one day's spike sets that cycle's bill.

*Part of the WhatsApp allowance expires early. 500,000 UK and 100,000 APAC are carryover from the previous term, due by 30 Nov 2026 per the Q-18818 proposal deck. Tableau cannot distinguish carryover from uplift, so the table shows the single allowance line.

Everything ends 14 Aug 2027 and does not auto-renew. Allowance can be added mid-term with 30 days' notice; it can't be reduced.
