Term and monthly allowances fail differently

Email, WhatsApp and push are one pot for the year. A heavy month is fine if the year totals under — which is why these need a forecast, not a monthly check.

Profiles, custom events and embedded messaging reset monthly, and profiles are a daily high-water mark, so one day's spike sets that month's bill and a quiet month can't offset it.

Everything ends 14 Aug 2027 and does not auto-renew. Allowance can be added mid-term with 30 days' notice; it can't be reduced.

Read these per monthly cycle, not contract-year-to-date

Email, WhatsApp and push are one pot for the whole term, so they are read from 15 Aug to date. Profiles and custom events are not — they reset on the 15th of each month and each cycle is billed on its own peak.

So the usage page should be set to one cycle at a time: the completed cycle for what was billed, and the cycle now running (15 Sep – 14 Oct) to catch a spike before the invoice. A range spanning more than one cycle returns the peak across all of them, which tells you nothing about what any single month cost.

Cycle 15 Aug – 14 Sep closed at 87.7% of the profile allowance and 65.7% of custom events. Nothing billed above allowance.
